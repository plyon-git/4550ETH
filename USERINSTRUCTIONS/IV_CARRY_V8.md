# Daily/weekly IV with overnight carry: V8

New independent research is in [hold72_iv_v8](../hold72_iv_v8/README.md). Read its [results](../hold72_iv_v8/RESULTS.md) and [instructions](../hold72_iv_v8/USERINSTRUCTIONS.md).

It implements a fixed initial 5R target, maximum 72h hold, daily/weekly historical IV-derived model bands and a 30x nominal contract setting with explicit account-risk and collateral accounting. It allows overnight positions and does not require daily entries.

An ETH diagnostic averages 55.83% arithmetically across five years but compounds at 19.75%, loses in three years and has only 363 trades. This is not a validated 45%-CAGR or consistent annual strategy. Original source and the V4 live adapter are unchanged; no V8 live orders were submitted. Project-specific work attributed to Parrish Lyon.
